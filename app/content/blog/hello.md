This is my corner of the internet — a place to point at the things I build and
occasionally write about why.

I tend to gravitate toward three things:

- **Systems made tangible.** The fastest way I learn something is to make it
  interactive and then break it on purpose. [OS Academy](https://learn.vamsikrishnapodipireddi.in)
  came out of that itch — schedule processes, watch page faults, trigger a race
  condition you can actually see.
- **Local-first AI.** I like keeping my data on my own hardware. Lekha reads my
  inbox with a local LLM, and my photo-restoration pipeline runs entirely on a
  Radeon GPU — no cloud, no API keys.
- **Small tools I use daily.** Like a one-line statusline for Claude Code that
  packs model, context usage, and plan limits into a single row.

Everything here ships with source, and a live link where it makes sense to click
around. Have a look at the [projects](/#projects), and feel free to
[reach out](mailto:podipireddivamsikrishna2001@gmail.com).
